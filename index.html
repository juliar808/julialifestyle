What you'll find here

Short write-ups on individual products and simple routines: what something does, what it feels like to use, and roughly what it costs. I write about what a product does rather than what's supposedly wrong with your face.

What I don't do

  • Claim a product treats acne, aging, or any skin condition. I'm not a dermatologist and neither is a product listing.
  • Give advice on combining active ingredients. Ask a professional.
  • Post before-and-after comparisons.
  • List prices, because they change constantly. Check the current price at the link.
