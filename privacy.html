Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

The short version

This is a personal website run by one person. It has no accounts, no login, no comments, no newsletter, and no tracking scripts. I don't collect personal information from visitors, and I have nothing to sell to anyone.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site is static pages with no forms and no analytics.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub records standard server request data such as IP addresses. That happens at the hosting layer and I have no access to it. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some links here and on my Pinterest boards go to Amazon and carry a tracking tag that tells Amazon the visit came from me.

If you click one, Amazon may place a cookie in your browser so a purchase can be attributed to my account. I don't see who you are — I only see aggregate counts of clicks and orders. What Amazon does with that data is governed by Amazon's privacy notice, not this one.

Buying through an affiliate link costs you nothing extra, and whether a product has an affiliate link has no bearing on whether I recommend it.

Pinterest

I publish to my own Pinterest business account using Pinterest's official API. That connection reads and writes only my own boards, pins, and analytics. It does not access any other Pinterest user's data, and I don't store information about people who view or save my pins beyond the aggregate counts Pinterest shows me in its own analytics.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and I don't knowingly collect information from them. Since the site collects nothing from anyone, there's nothing to delete — but if you have a concern, email me.

Your choices

  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, which will stop affiliate attribution.
  • You can browse this site with any content blocker; nothing here depends on tracking.
  • You can email me with any question about this policy.

Changes

If I add anything that collects data — an email list, analytics, ads — I'll update this page and change the date at the top.
